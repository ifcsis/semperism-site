The author

S. Harrison Knudson, J.D.

American lawyer and scholar, Arlington, Virginia.

Steele Knudson has some two decades of practice across federal regulatory compliance, export controls and anti-corruption, antitrust, land use and internet governance. He holds a J.D. from George Mason University’s Antonin Scalia Law School and a B.S. in Urban Planning from the University of Utah.

His work runs on several tracks that share one question — how institutions come to exceed the terms on which they were founded. On the constitutional side that is Semperism. On the technical side it is the design of a protected namespace for children, pursued through the International Foundation for Child-Safe internet Standards, where he is Director.

Correspondence: shknudson@ifcsis.org
