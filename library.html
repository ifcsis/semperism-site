SE-03 · Article

The Key of the Constitution

Purpose, oath, and the republic that may not be consecrated

The Preamble states what the government is for, the oath states what officers swear to, and the Constitution refuses to say what any citizen must profess. One design: a purpose statement is as confining as a rights statement.

SE-04 · Essay

The Caged Constitution

Why the Semperists were right

The men called Anti-Federalists were not against the Constitution. They were against trusting structure to hold without written prohibitions. Madison came to agree, and wrote the cage himself.

SE-05 · Working draft

Conscience to the Church; Obligation to the State

Marriage as incorporation

The state has two powers over domestic life: to enforce obligations and to define relationships. It should keep the first and give up the second. Churches keep marriage; the state registers what people actually undertake.

Forum Shopping for the Apocalypse

AI eschatology as a jurisdictional maneuver

Whether it promises extinction or paradise, AI eschatology works as a forum-selection clause. It moves authority up to summits and licensing boards, and away from the ordinary law states and county courts already enforce.

SE-01 · Essay

Who Pays the Bill?

A Semperist reading of the European Return Regulation

Europe’s return debate asks who must leave. It does not ask who owes for the displacement in the first place. The receiving state has already answered that question in its own recognition decisions, and the answer names a regime.

The Jurisprudence of Voluntary Risk

When institutions may penalise lawful private conduct

Smoking, cannabis, motorcycles, firearms: employers, insurers, civil services and the military each treat lawful risk-taking differently, and no one designed the pattern. A test for when an institution may reach conduct the law permits.

Why the full papers are sent rather than posted

Several of these papers are still moving, and some are written for a particular audience or jurisdiction. Rather than post a version written for nobody in particular, I send the current one with the question in view. Say what you are working on in the request and I will reply with the paper, usually within a few days. Requests are as welcome from critics as from allies.
