SE-01 · Essay

Who Pays the Bill?

A Semperist reading of the European Return Regulation

Europe’s return debate asks who must leave. It does not ask who owes for the displacement in the first place. The receiving state has already answered that question in its own recognition decisions, and the answer names a regime.

SE-02 · Outline

The Jurisprudence of Voluntary Risk

When institutions may penalise lawful private conduct

Smoking, cannabis, motorcycles, firearms: employers, insurers, civil services and the military each treat lawful risk-taking differently, and no one designed the pattern. A test for when an institution may reach conduct the law permits.

Why the full papers are sent rather than posted

Several of these papers are still moving, and some are written for a particular audience or jurisdiction. Rather than post a version written for nobody in particular, I send the current one with the question in view. Say what you are working on in the request and I will reply with the paper, usually within a few days. Requests are as welcome from critics as from allies.
